# VB FinCalc — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Developer:** VB InsureSathi

VB InsureSathi ("we," "our," or "us") built the **VB FinCalc** app as a financial calculator tool. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

## 1. No Account or Login Required

VB FinCalc does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. You can use all features of the application anonymously without providing personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, email address, or government ID.

## 2. Data Collection and Local Storage

- **Local Storage Only:** Any financial inputs, calculation histories, or preferences you enter into Financial Sathi are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage.
- **No External Transmission:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or process your financial calculations or personal data on any external servers or cloud databases.
- **Control Over Your Data:** Because your data resides exclusively on your device, you have complete control over it. You can delete your saved calculations at any time through the app (where applicable), by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the application.

## 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties. Since no personal or financial data leaves your device, third parties have no access to the information you enter into the calculator.

> **Note:** If the app is distributed through platforms such as the Google Play Store, the platform provider may independently collect standard, aggregated technical diagnostics or crash reports according to their own privacy policies.

## 4. Security

Because your data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on the security of your device itself. We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and securing your device with a passcode, PIN, or biometric lock.

## 5. Children's Privacy

VB FinCalc does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
